# Money Lego

Ask about your PayPal sandbox money and get a workflow built for anything that moves it.

- **Look things up in PayPal**: unpaid invoices, orders, refunds, disputes, subscriptions and transactions, through PayPal's official Agent Toolkit, read-only.
- **Explain failures**: why a payout or a workflow run failed, from the run history.
- **Summarise money**: captured, paid out and refunded totals from Money Lego's ledger.
- **Build workflows**: describe an automation ("every Monday, remind clients about overdue invoices after I approve") and Money Lego's crew (planner, guard, fixer, connector) drafts it. It's saved turned off, and every money step waits for your approval.

The agent never moves money itself. It acts as one Money Lego account and sees only that account's workflows, runs and PayPal objects.
